# Jeel — Experience & Mentorship Platform

## About the Project

Jeel is a mobile mentorship application designed to support knowledge and experience transfer between generations.

The application connects students, young adults, and young professionals with experienced individuals and retired professionals who are willing to share their knowledge and real-world experience.

## Problem

Many young individuals need practical guidance when making academic, career, or professional decisions. At the same time, experienced professionals possess valuable knowledge that may not be effectively transferred to younger generations.

Jeel provides a structured platform that connects these two groups.

## Target Users

- Mentors: Experienced and retired professionals.
- Mentees: University students, graduates, young professionals, and individuals seeking practical guidance.

## Core Features

- User Registration & Role Selection
- Mentor and Mentee Profiles
- Mentor Search and Discovery
- Mentor Matching
- Mentorship Requests
- Session Booking
- Session Management
- Challenge Submission
- Action Plan & Progress Tracking
- Ratings & Feedback
- Notifications
